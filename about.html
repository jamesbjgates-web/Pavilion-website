About Pavilion

Commercial support built around the realities of construction.

Pavilion helps construction businesses make better-informed commercial decisions — from identifying opportunities and reviewing contracts to managing project issues, resolving disputes and recovering payment.

Understand the facts. Understand the commercial position. Decide what happens next.

Experience that predates statutory adjudication.

Pavilion's services draw on 30 years' experience in the construction sector. That experience began before the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 introduced the framework for statutory construction adjudication.

That depth of experience combines practical construction knowledge with an understanding of contracts, commercial disputes, payment issues and adjudication — helping Pavilion approach problems in the context of how construction projects actually operate.

Understand the problem before trying to sell the solution.

Construction problems rarely arrive neatly packaged. Pavilion starts by establishing the facts and understanding the context. Only then can the appropriate commercial response be considered.

Evidence matters.

Whether assessing a prospect, reviewing a tender or examining a construction dispute, Pavilion looks for evidence that supports a conclusion — and evidence that challenges it. The objective isn't to tell a client what they want to hear. It's to give them a clearer commercial picture on which to make a decision.

Technology with judgement.

Technology can find information. Judgement determines whether it matters. Pavilion uses modern research and analytical tools where they improve quality or efficiency, but commercial decisions are not delegated blindly to software.

Better information. Clearer commercial decisions.
