EXPERIENCE IN CONTEXT

We've seen the industry change.

Thirty years in construction spans a great deal of change. Procurement has changed. Contracts have become increasingly complex. Technology has transformed how opportunities are found, tenders are issued and projects are administered.

But many of the pressures facing contractors remain remarkably familiar: finding the right work, understanding what you're signing, protecting entitlement, maintaining cash flow and getting paid properly for the work you've done.

Pavilion exists to help with those pressures.

HOW WE THINK

Support should be accessible.

Smaller construction businesses often face exactly the same commercial pressures as much larger organisations, but without an estimating department, commercial team, in-house legal support or the financial resources to absorb expensive professional fees.

01

We work with people, not just accounts.

Entry costs should be sensible and, where appropriate, success-fee arrangements can allow Pavilion to share some of the risk.

02

Evidence before assumption.

We distinguish fact from assumption, identify what isn't yet known and say when something doesn't stack up.

03

Straight talking.

We'll tell you what we think, where the uncertainties lie and when legal advice or another specialist is required.

Good commercial advice isn't always the answer you hoped to hear. It should be the answer the evidence supports.
