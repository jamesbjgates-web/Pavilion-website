About Pavilion

Commercial support built on construction experience.

Pavilion provides practical commercial support to construction businesses — helping them find worthwhile opportunities, understand contractual and commercial risk, and recover the money they've earned.

Our services draw on 30 years' experience in the construction sector, combining practical industry knowledge with structured research, evidence and clear commercial thinking.

We've seen the industry change.

Thirty years in construction spans a great deal of change. Procurement has changed. Contracts have become increasingly complex. The Construction Act introduced statutory payment provisions and adjudication. Technology has transformed how opportunities are found, tenders are issued and projects are administered.

But many of the pressures facing contractors remain remarkably familiar: finding the right work, understanding what you're signing, protecting entitlement, maintaining cash flow and getting paid properly for the work you've done.

Pavilion exists to help with those pressures.

Practical experience matters.

Problems rarely arrive neatly packaged.

A tender opportunity can contain a contractual risk. A variation can become a valuation disagreement. A valuation disagreement can become a payment problem. A payment problem can become a dispute.

Pavilion looks at the commercial position as a whole, rather than treating every problem as though it belongs in a separate box.

Support should be accessible.

Smaller construction businesses often face exactly the same commercial pressures as much larger organisations, but without an estimating department, commercial team, in-house legal support or the financial resources to absorb expensive professional fees.

Pavilion believes that shouldn't prevent a good business from getting good commercial support. That's why we aim to keep entry costs sensible, explain fees clearly and, in appropriate payment and dispute matters, consider success-fee arrangements that allow Pavilion to share some of the risk with the client.

We work with people, not just accounts.

Evidence before assumption.

Whether we're assessing a prospect, reviewing a tender, examining a contractual position or progressing a payment, we want to understand what the evidence actually supports. That means distinguishing fact from assumption, identifying what isn't yet known and being prepared to say when something doesn't stack up.

Good commercial advice isn't always the answer you hoped to hear. It should be the answer the evidence supports.

Technology where it helps. People where they matter.

Modern research and analytical tools allow information to be found, organised and examined faster than was previously possible. Pavilion uses technology where it improves the quality and efficiency of our work — but commercial decisions aren't simply handed over to an algorithm.

Material recommendations are reviewed by people.

Straight talking.

We'll tell you what we think the position is, what the evidence supports, where the uncertainties lie and what we think you should do next. If something falls outside Pavilion's expertise or requires legal advice or another specialist, we'll tell you that too.

Pavilion is not a firm of solicitors and does not provide reserved legal services. Where a matter requires legal advice or representation, we'll recommend that appropriate professional support is obtained.

Tell us what you're trying to achieve.

You don't need to know which Pavilion service you need before getting in touch. Tell us about the opportunity, contract, commercial problem or unpaid account and we'll help identify an appropriate starting point.
