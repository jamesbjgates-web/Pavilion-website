EARLIER IS BETTER

Disputes are always better avoided.

Commercial problems frequently begin much earlier than the dispute itself: an onerous provision accepted at tender stage, a notice not issued, an instruction not properly recorded, a variation allowed to drift, or a payment position left unclear.

Pavilion helps contractors understand and manage those issues while there is still an opportunity to do something about them.

The best time to identify an onerous contract provision is before the contract is signed — not afterwards, when you're left to suffer its consequences.
