Commercial & Dispute Support

Get control of the commercial position before it controls you.

When a commercial issue arises on a construction project, acting early can matter. Pavilion can help establish the facts, review the contractual position, identify notice and record-keeping requirements, organise the commercial record and determine what needs to happen next.

A problem does not need to have become a dispute before it deserves attention.

Where Pavilion can help

Before work starts

Pre-Contract Commercial Review

Review proposed contract documents, identify provisions that warrant attention and help understand their practical implications before the contract is entered into.

While work is live

Live Project Commercial Support

Review emerging issues, notice provisions and timescales, contemporaneous records and commercial correspondence.

When positions diverge

Commercial Position & Dispute Review

Bring together the contract, notices, correspondence, applications, valuations, instructions, programmes and records to establish what happened and identify strengths or gaps in the evidence.

Payment & Valuation Disputes

Applications, certificates, payment notices, pay-less notices, variations, deductions, retention and final accounts.

Variations & Additional Works

Instructions, scope changes, additional works and the records supporting the resulting commercial position.

Delay, Disruption & Loss and Expense

Delay events, extensions of time, disruption, correspondence, programmes, notices and supporting records.

Final Accounts

Outstanding variations, valuations, contra-charges, retention, claims and unresolved commercial matters.

Construction Adjudication
