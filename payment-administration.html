Commercial Payment & Recovery

You've done the work. You shouldn't have to carry the debt.

You have priced the work, committed the resources and incurred the cost. When payment becomes due, it should be paid. Pavilion helps construction businesses take control of overdue and disputed payments.

Don't wait for an overdue account to become an old debt.

Pavilion can become involved as soon as a payment issue emerges — reviewing applications, invoices, certificates, payment notices, pay-less notices, retentions, variations and correspondence to establish exactly where the account stands.

If money is due, we help pursue it. If the amount is disputed, we identify why.

Late payment has a cost.

Where the Late Payment of Commercial Debts legislation applies, Pavilion can calculate statutory interest and compensation and incorporate those amounts into a clear schedule of the debt.

Recovery with purpose.

Every communication should move the account towards payment. Pavilion can manage a structured recovery process and maintain a clear record throughout.

When “we haven't paid” becomes “we don't owe it”.

When an overdue account becomes a commercial dispute, Pavilion can examine the underlying project records and contractual position rather than simply repeating the demand for payment.

If correspondence doesn't produce payment, the matter doesn't simply stop.
