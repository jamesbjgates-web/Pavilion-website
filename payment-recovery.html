FROM STALLED PAYMENT TO CLEAR ACTION

Getting paid shouldn't require endless chasing.

You've carried out the work. Submitted the application or invoice. Supplied the information requested. And then payment stalls. Pavilion brings structure to the position — establishing what is due, what evidence supports it and what should happen next.

01

Review

Establish what was applied for or invoiced, what has been paid, what remains outstanding and what the contract and relevant notices say.

02

Structure

Bring together applications, invoices, valuations, variations, correspondence, notices and supporting evidence.

03

Calculate

Establish the amount properly due and, where applicable, statutory interest and compensation.

04

Progress

Present the position clearly and pursue it through an appropriate sequence of correspondence and follow-up.
