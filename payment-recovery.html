Payment & Recovery

Money earned should be in your bank.

Practical support to establish what is due, organise the evidence and progress overdue payments — avoiding employers taking advantage of undue credit.

Getting paid shouldn't require endless chasing.

You've carried out the work. Submitted the application or invoice. Supplied the information requested. And then payment stalls.

Emails go unanswered. Promised payment dates pass. Queries appear that could have been raised weeks earlier.

Your business isn't there to provide somebody else's working capital.

Pavilion helps bring structure to the position — establishing what is due, what evidence supports it, what needs addressing and what should happen next.

01 · Review

Establish what was applied for or invoiced, what has been paid, what remains outstanding and what the contract and relevant notices say.

02 · Structure

Bring together applications, invoices, valuations, variations, correspondence, notices and other supporting evidence.

03 · Calculate

Establish the amount properly due and, where applicable, calculate statutory interest and compensation under late-payment legislation.

04 · Progress

Present the position clearly and pursue it through an appropriate sequence of correspondence and follow-up.

Escalation should be deliberate, not automatic.

No recovery. No Pavilion success fee.

Where appropriate, Pavilion can undertake both straightforward and disputed payment recovery on an agreed success-fee basis. Rather than requiring substantial professional fees upfront, Pavilion shares the risk with you. Our agreed fee is calculated as a percentage of sums successfully recovered.

Straightforward overdue payment

Where entitlement is reasonably established and the principal issue is non-payment, Pavilion may offer recovery at an agreed percentage of sums recovered.

Disputed payment

Where entitlement, valuation, variations, final account or another aspect of the payment is disputed, Pavilion may still offer a success-fee arrangement following an initial assessment of the position, evidence and work required.

Initial assessment

No charge. We'll review the circumstances sufficiently to understand the problem and determine whether Pavilion is prepared to take the matter on at its own risk.

Third-party costs are separate. Pavilion's success-fee arrangement relates to Pavilion's professional fees only. Adjudicator's fees, court fees, solicitor's fees, barrister's fees, expert fees and other external costs or disbursements remain the client's responsibility and are payable separately. Pavilion will discuss material external costs with you before they are incurred.
Construction adjudication. Where Pavilion agrees to support an adjudication on a success-fee basis, the success-fee arrangement will cover Pavilion's professional time. The adjudicator's fees and any other external costs remain payable by the client, irrespective of the outcome.

Firm doesn't have to mean confrontational.

The objective is payment. Sometimes a clear reconciliation and properly evidenced request is enough. Sometimes persistent follow-up is required. Sometimes the position needs formal escalation. Pavilion matches the response to the problem while maintaining a clear recovery record.

Sometimes the problem isn't simply that somebody hasn't paid.

If the amount itself is disputed because of valuation, variations, contractual entitlement, contra-charges, defects, set-off or the final account, Pavilion can examine the underlying commercial position as part of the recovery strategy.

The objective isn't to create proceedings. The objective is to get you paid.
