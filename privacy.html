Privacy & Data

Your information should be handled with the same care as your commercial interests.

Information you give us

When you contact Pavilion or become a client, we may receive business contact details, company information and details about the service or matter concerned. Depending on the work, clients may also provide commercially sensitive project material.

Please don't send confidential project documents through the general website enquiry form.

Research and intelligence

Pavilion's Prospect and Tender Intelligence services may involve researching companies, projects, procurement opportunities and relevant business contacts using publicly available and appropriately accessible sources, combined where appropriate with client criteria.

The purpose is commercial research and decision support — not the creation or sale of indiscriminate contact lists.

Client Tender Profiles

Information supplied in a Client Tender Profile is used to understand the client's capabilities, preferences and commercial requirements so potential procurement opportunities can be assessed against them.

Accuracy and judgement

Publicly available information is not always complete or current. Intelligence reports are decision-support material rather than a guarantee that every underlying fact remains current or complete.

Your information is not a product.

Pavilion does not sell client information or use confidential client material to create marketing lists for third parties.

Data protection

Pavilion (Stockport) Ltd processes personal information for appropriate business purposes connected with enquiries, client engagements, service delivery, administration, legal obligations and appropriately conducted business research, using an appropriate lawful basis. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required.

Individuals may have rights under UK data-protection law and may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. For privacy enquiries contact hello@pspt.ltd.

Cookies and tracking

Pavilion aims to keep website tracking to a minimum and does not require advertising trackers or behavioural profiling to provide its core services.
