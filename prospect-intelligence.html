Prospect Intelligence

Fewer prospects. Much better researched.

Prospect Intelligence finds businesses and projects worth approaching. Tender Intelligence finds procurement opportunities worth considering.

The objective isn't to produce the longest list. It's to identify a smaller number of businesses genuinely worth your sales team's time — and provide the evidence explaining why.

We don't just give you a name. We give you a reason to make the call.

This isn't a bought database, a scraped contact list or an unexplained AI score. Each prospect is researched individually against an agreed commercial profile. We look for evidence that supports the opportunity — and deliberately look for evidence that could rule it out.

01 · Discover

Find businesses showing genuine commercial or project signals.

02 · Challenge

Actively search for reasons the opportunity may not be worth pursuing.

03 · Research

Build the commercial, operational and timing picture — including who may be involved in the decision.

04 · Assess

Evaluate opportunity strength, accessibility and confidence in the evidence.

05 · Deliver

Provide the intelligence, source trail, suggested approach and questions that still need answering.
