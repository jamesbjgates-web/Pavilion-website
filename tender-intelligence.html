Tender Intelligence

Your Tender Profile defines what makes an opportunity right for your business. Tender Intelligence helps find it.

We compare public procurement opportunities against your capabilities, preferred work, geography, contract values and commercial criteria — and look for reasons not to bid before you commit estimating resources.

Find fewer tenders. Find better tenders.

Tender portals can provide large numbers of opportunities. The problem is deciding which ones deserve your attention. Pavilion searches for opportunities that fit your business, then examines the available tender information against the profile you have given us.

We are not simply looking for reasons to include an opportunity. We actively look for reasons to exclude it.

Eligibility Gate

Check mandatory requirements and conditions first.

Tender Fit

Assess scope, geography, value, experience, timing and procurement fit.

Evidence & Gaps

Identify relevant capability and questions still requiring an answer.

The tender you don't price can be as valuable as the one you win.

Preparing a serious construction tender consumes time and resources. Tender Intelligence is designed to help concentrate those resources on opportunities with a credible reason to pursue them — while identifying poor-fit opportunities as early as possible.
