How it works

Your Tender Profile tells us what makes an opportunity right for your business.

Pavilion uses the profile to compare live and emerging procurement opportunities against your capabilities, preferred work, geography, contract values, commercial appetite and track record.

Eligibility GateMandatory requirements, accreditations, turnover limits and other conditions are checked first.
Tender FitScope, geography, value, experience, timing and procurement fit are assessed against the client profile.
ChallengeWe actively look for reasons the opportunity may not justify bid resource.
Evidence & GapsRelevant experience and capability are identified, together with questions that still need answering.
