More tender alerts aren't necessarily more opportunities.

Construction businesses can already access thousands of procurement notices. The difficult part isn't finding them. It's working out whether the opportunity is genuinely right, whether you are capable and eligible, what the tender documentation actually requires, what commercial or contractual issues matter, and whether it deserves estimating and management time.

01 · Discover

Identify potentially relevant opportunities and check current procurement information.

02 · Qualify

Test the published opportunity against your Tender Profile and known barriers.

03 · Investigate

Go beyond the headline notice into tender documents, requirements and evidence.

04 · Challenge

Deliberately look for reasons not to pursue as well as reasons to pursue.

05 · Recommend

Provide a clear evidence-led recommendation and next steps.

Finding reasons not to bid can be just as valuable as finding reasons to bid.
