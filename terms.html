Clear commercial support. Clear professional boundaries.

Pavilion provides commercial support, research, analysis, payment and construction adjudication services to businesses operating in and around the construction sector.

Commercial and construction services

Pavilion may assist with pre-contract commercial reviews, live-project commercial issues, payment and valuation matters, construction disputes and adjudication.

Pavilion (Stockport) Ltd is not a firm of solicitors and does not provide reserved legal services. Where a matter requires reserved legal services or specialist legal representation, Pavilion may recommend appropriate professional advice.

Prospect & Tender Intelligence

Pavilion's intelligence services support commercial decision-making using public and appropriately accessible sources assessed against agreed client criteria.

An opportunity identified by Pavilion is not a guarantee of suitability, availability, contract award or commercial success. Clients remain responsible for deciding whether to approach a prospect, participate in a procurement process or submit a tender.

Information and evidence

Public sources may be incomplete, inaccurate or subsequently changed. Where appropriate, Pavilion may indicate the source and confidence of information.

Client decisions

Pavilion provides information, analysis and commercial support. The commercial decision remains the client's.
