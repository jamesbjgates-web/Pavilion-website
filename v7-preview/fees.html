HOW FEES WORK

Supportive doesn't mean open-ended.

A “from” price isn't permission for an open-ended bill. Where substantial work falls outside the agreed scope, we'll discuss it before undertaking additional chargeable work.

If cash flow is the problem, talk to us.

Where appropriate, we'll look for a fee structure that allows the problem to be addressed rather than making it worse.
